      why us?

      the team

      Green Light Studio is a compact, experienced marketing implementation team that helps clarify your strategy and carry enough of the load so marketing becomes manageable and measurable again.


      Sound like a team you want to work with?

      • Who we work with

        We work with industrial manufacturers, commercial companies, NGOs, and purpose-driven organizations across the U.S., Europe, and Southeast Asia. Most of our clients have long sales cycles, distributor networks, and technical products where clarity matters more than hype.


        We don’t work with startups, tech, crypto, or AI companies. Nothing wrong with them, we just don't match their needs.

      • How we work with you

        We work best alongside an in-house marketing lead or small internal team. Your team owns marketing internally—we provide dependable backup. 


        That includes strategic thinking, execution support, and day-to-day help that reduces pressure instead of creating more work to manage.

      • What do we actually do?

        • Annual marketing plans
        • Video editing
        • Podcast editing
        • Social media
        • Branding
        • Newsletters
        • Sales Promotion designs
        • Video case studies
        • International dealer marketing support
        • Article writing
        • YouTube channel management
        • 3D product visualization
        • Website upgrades
        • Google/Facebook ads
        • Influencer marketing 

      • What's it cost?

        Because our experienced team works remotely and efficiently, our rates offer significant value compared to hiring equivalent roles in the U.S. or Europe—without compromising quality, ownership, or accountability.


        Typical engagements start around $3,500 monthly.

      • Nothing off-the-shelf

        We don't grab a 'social media template' and apply it to your brand and 20 other brands. 


        Everything we do is tailored to your business needs. 


        We adapt to your market, your internal constraints, and the reality of how your company actually operates, and most importantly, what your customers expect.

      • Tightknit by design

        Our team is intentionally small. We hire carefully, train seriously, and work with a limited number of clients at a time. Everyone on the team takes pride in their work and is accountable for what they deliver.


        Between us, we speak nine languages and work comfortably across time zones.

      • A long-term vision

        We don’t do one-off projects or short engagements. Our work is built around long-term partnerships where strategy, systems, and execution improve steadily over time.


        Most clients stay with us for years because the work reduces pressure, increases confidence in what's working, and actually moves the business forward.
