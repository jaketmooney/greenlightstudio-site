### **The Challenge**  

Boklua View Resort is a small, family-run retreat tucked into the mountains of Northern Thailand. Set amid forests, rice terraces, and creeks, it’s a place built on intention—from the architecture to the food to the deep care for the land and community.

  

But that didn’t come through strongly online.  

![](./902644ee.png)

![](./60cf35ee.png)

![](./cd2bb7f9.png)

![](./59761c18.png)

The resort’s old website lacked high-quality visuals and didn’t reflect the true guest experience. There was no video to show the guest experience and communicate the founder’s story—one of returning home, preserving local traditions, and building something lasting for future generations.  

> They didn’t have very good advertising, very good photos—nothing that really showed what they had to offer. They needed video.... so we made a video.
>
> — Richard

* * *

### **Our Approach**  

Rather than create a promotional ad, Green Light Studio set out to document what makes Boklua View meaningful—to tell a real story in a way that visitors could see and feel before ever arriving.

We filmed on location over several days, working with the natural light, the rhythm of daily life, and the landscape itself. Despite weather challenges—cloud cover and rain—we stayed flexible, capturing drone footage and interior scenes when the moments allowed.  

![](./f30b5f99.png)

> This was one of my favorite projects. The location is so beautiful. Getting to show that on film—and to see it all come together in the final product—was incredibly satisfying.
>
> — Richard

The video includes shots of the Maeng River, the original rice terraces, guest rooms, and the local kitchen. The narration, paired with English captions, tells the founder’s story in his own words: why he left Bangkok, why he came back, and why the resort is more than a business—it’s a way to sustain the community without giving up its soul.

![](./ce9a5f01.png)

* * *

**What We Delivered**

*   **A 7-minute video** in Thai with English captions, ideal for the website homepage and social sharing
*   **Drone and on-the-ground footage** of the rooms, gardens, natural surroundings, and local food
*   **A personal, founder-led narrative** that balances aspiration with authenticity

> The collaboration with the client made the food shots special. He’s a chef, and we were able to show the dishes with real care and intention. That brought a lot to the story.
>
> — Richard
